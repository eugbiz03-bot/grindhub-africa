Privacy Policy

Welcome to GrindHub Africa! Your trust matters to us. Here's how we handle your information:

  • 1. What data we collect

    We do not require you to sign up or create an account. If you voluntarily submit your email and message through our contact form, we receive that information. We also use StatCounter, a website analytics tool, which collects general, non-identifying visit data such as your approximate location, browser type, and which pages you viewed — this helps us understand how people use the site. It does not collect your name or email unless you choose to give it to us directly.

  • 2. How we use your data

    Any email or message you send us is used only to respond to your inquiries or provide the information you request. We do not share or sell your information to anyone.

  • 3. Cookies and Tracking

    We use StatCounter for basic site analytics, which may use cookies to measure visits. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies, and we do not sell any data to advertisers. You can read more about how analytics cookies generally work via Google's cookie policy as a general reference.

  • 4. How we protect your data

    We keep your contact details secure and use them only for communication purposes.

  • 5. Your rights

    You can ask us to delete your information anytime by contacting us directly.

  • 6. Updates to this policy

    We may update this policy occasionally. Check back here to stay informed.
