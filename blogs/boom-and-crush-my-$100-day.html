Boom & Crash: My $100 Day

Posted by Eugene | December 2025

I still remember that day clearly — it started with just $20 in my account. I wasn’t even sure what I was doing yet, but I had been studying sniper entries like crazy. One thing i learnt about trading is that it's requires a lot of patients, for years i have been practicing in my demo account, When it was time to transit to my real account all i did was to use the same straget i used in my demo.

I waited patiently for a strong rejection candle on Crash 500, set my lot size to 0.02, and boom — I got in. Minutes later, the spike hit exactly where I expected. Cause i was confident enough that it will go where i wanted and thanks to heavy studies it did exactly i wanted.

🧠 The Strategy

My entry rules were simple:

  • Wait for price to hit previous resistance
  • Look for engulfing or pin bar
  • Only enter if RSI was over 75 or under 25

And it worked — over and over again that day. I hit $134 by evening and felt like I had just hacked the matrix.

📉 The Lesson

What made it real wasn’t the profit — it was the patience. I saw trades forming and didn’t jump in. I followed the plan.

This wasn't about luck. This was the day I realized: **discipline makes the difference**.

One thing about all this stuff is consistent, yes yo consistent is the key find what works what doesn't work and repeat, Yes keep going homie.

🔥 Final Words

If you're out there grinding like me, just know you're not alone. We fall, We fail but most important is deciding what to do when things like that hit you. Do me a favor stand up, keep moving.
