Broker Overview

Deriv

Deriv is best known for it's synthetic indices like Boom and Crash, which operate 24/7 and are not affected by news or real-world marked events . This makes Deriv extremely popular among traders in Africa.

Exness

Exness is a globally recognized broker offering forex, gold, crypto, indices, and more. It supports MT4 and MT5 and is regulated, making it attractive for traders who want a real-market experience.

Exness Instruments

Minimum Deposit & Small Capital Friendliness

For beginners, starting small is not weakness — it’s intelligence.

Exness allows very small real deposits depending on payment method, making it beginner-friendly for traders testing the waters with real money.

Deriv also supports low deposits, but beginners often struggle because demo trading with $10,000 doesn’t mentally prepare them for small real accounts.
